# GTM — App Store / Apple Search Ads

Use only for products distributed in the relevant store and when store search intent exists.

## Course template
geo; store page; daily budget (course example range $5–50/day); keyword list; popularity/demand; one keyword/group for clearer analysis where useful.

## Current-platform gate
Verify current Apple Search Ads campaign types, keyword metrics, bidding and policy. Store-page conversion and product readiness are part of the experiment.
