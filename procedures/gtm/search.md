# GTM — Search (Google/Yandex)

## Use when
People search for the solution/product and there are hot/transitional queries. Competitor presence in search is a positive signal if CPC/economics are viable.

## Keyword protocol
- collect a broad candidate set using research tools;
- keep transactional/hot intent, reject informational intent;
- include short + long-tail terms when intent is clear;
- course workflow often reviews ~10–20 candidates and launches ~4–5 focused keywords;
- course demand heuristics: group of ~5–6 keywords around ~3k monthly searches for B2B, ~15k for B2C. Treat as rough heuristic only;
- attach volume, CPC forecast, competition where available.

## Message match
`keyword meaning ↔ ad headline/description ↔ landing promise` must align.

## Plan
geo; platform; landing; daily budget; keywords; negative keywords; ads; conversion event; analytics; CPC forecast.

## After launch: search-specific monitoring
In addition to impressions/clicks/conversions, inspect:
1. **actual search terms / conditions of display** — what queries really triggered ads;
2. **traffic/impression share** — how much of available demand the ad captures;
3. **position/top impression share** — whether ads are actually visible high enough.

Use those to distinguish bad semantics from low rank/budget and from landing/sales failure.

## Diagnosis order
Search terms quality → impression volume/share → position/top share → CTR → landing leads → qualified leads → payments. Add negatives and narrow semantic scope before rewriting the whole product.
