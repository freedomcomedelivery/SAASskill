# GTM — Meta / paid social

## Use when
The product is not primarily searched or media discovery is appropriate and the avatar is broad enough to be found algorithmically. Source materials frame Meta as especially practical for many B2C cases, but do not treat that as exclusive.

## Simple early funnel
Prefer simple direct paths: ad → landing/lead form/trial/message → qualification/activation → payment. Retargeting/lookalikes/complex multi-step systems are scaling tools, not the first requirement.

## Preparation
- one avatar and one offer;
- detailed mobile-first landing or native lead flow;
- meaningful conversion event/dataset/pixel when current platform supports it;
- competitor creative research in current ad libraries;
- UTM/source naming so downstream lead quality can be tied to creative/ad set.

## Creative protocol from source
- derive claims from the landing/marketing contract, not invented ad-only promises;
- personalize to avatar/situation;
- use pain/problem, primary benefit, mechanism, proof or case as hooks;
- keep copy simple; visual design is secondary to clear offer;
- 2–3 ads per hypothesis/ad set is a source pattern, not a current platform rule;
- creative should filter non-avatar, not maximize cheap clicks.

## Course diagnostics
The Meta supplement contains historical teaching ranges (e.g. CTR bands, weekly conversion-count targets). Store them as `course_heuristic` only. Never automatically fail/pass a current campaign on those numbers without current account/market context.

## Current-platform gate
Meta UI, datasets/pixels, campaign objectives, Advantage+ behavior, account policies and targeting change frequently. Verify official current docs/live connector capabilities before any write.

The source also contains tactics involving purchased/farmed accounts, proxies and anti-detect tooling. These are **not executable instructions for this skill**. Do not perform account evasion, identity deception, fake activity or ToS bypass. Use compliant accounts and supported access only.

## Diagnosis
technical delivery/tracking → reach/CPM → creative response → landing page views → site conversion → leads → qualified leads → payments → CAC. CTR alone is not success.
