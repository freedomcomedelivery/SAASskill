# GTM — Outreach

## Use when
Target accounts/avatars are identifiable and relatively limited; course router uses <~10k avatars as a rough signal.

## Base
Course template expects at least ~100 relevant contacts for an initial batch and a source for those contacts. Quality of prospecting outranks raw volume.

## Message
Subject/hook should look human and relevant, not generic mass mail. Body: pain/context → mechanism → primary benefit → concrete offer/CTA; price when appropriate to market.

## Course benchmarks
Examples mention open rate ~40–70% and reply ~10–20% as good outcomes in some western campaigns. Treat as historical examples, not targets guaranteed today.

## Compliance
Respect current anti-spam/privacy rules and platform/email policies. Do not fabricate identities, evade sending limits or scrape prohibited personal data.
