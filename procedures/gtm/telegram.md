# GTM — Telegram placements

## Channel discovery
Find channels that plausibly contain the avatar, then filter.

## Course fields
price; subscribers; 24h reach; ERR; ad response/history when available; author-led vs repost/public feed; audience acquisition source; posting frequency; expected placement economics.

## Preference
Course strongly prefers author/influencer channels over repost farms because real audience attention is the objective.

## Creative
Hook must identify/interest avatar and repel non-avatar. Keep pain, solution, offer and landing consistent.

## Landing
Simple, contact-capturing, with follow-up path. Bot can be landing if appropriate.

## Experiment
Plan expected impressions/clicks/leads; buy a small set of placements; compare observed funnel, not subscriber count.
