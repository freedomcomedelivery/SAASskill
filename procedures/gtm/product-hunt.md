# GTM — Product Hunt / Product Radar

## Role
Additive channel. If target audience is not on the platform, do a lightweight launch for discovery/backlink rather than spending major project energy.

## Card
Direct tagline describing product or benefit; quality visuals; clear mechanism. Avoid vague clever tagline when unknown brand.

## Launch preparation (course)
Account activity/warm-up can help; choose date intentionally; hunter can matter; use tagged links/UTM; mobilize legitimate existing audience/community.

## Rule
No fake voting/engagement. Verify current platform anti-manipulation rules before launch.
