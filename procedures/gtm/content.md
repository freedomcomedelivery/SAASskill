# GTM — Content / short video

## Role
Low-cash, high-effort media channel. Course routes it as a media alternative when paid budget is constrained.

## Strategy
1. choose platform where algorithmic discovery exists;
2. research formats competitors already use;
3. produce multiple cheap tests;
4. measure retention/views/click-through and downstream qualified actions;
5. when a format wins, make many controlled variants instead of reinventing every post.

Course emphasizes short video because discovery feeds give new content a chance to receive initial reach.

Do not confuse views with validated demand; connect content to offer/landing/payment.
