# Avatar width

The course treats avatar width as an economic design variable, not a branding exercise.

## Narrow avatar
Pros from source materials: easier to identify, higher check can be possible, faster/cleaner feedback, direct outreach may work well. Cons: hard to find leads, long decision cycle, limited scale.

## Broad avatar
Pros: many acquisition channels and larger scale. Cons: lower relevance per impression, often lower check and more noise; traffic can initially only break even.

## Operator rule
Choose the **narrowest avatar that still has enough reachable demand for the selected channel**. Do not broaden avatar merely to increase TAM. If narrow market is identifiable, outreach may be better than media.

Record avatar width as a hypothesis tied to channel economics. Do not assign universal conversion rates from the Miro chart as facts.
