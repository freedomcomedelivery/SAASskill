# Lead onboarding

Goal: turn traffic into money and learn who is actually qualified.

## Funnel inside onboarding
`new lead → qualified lead → activated lead → paywall/offer → paying user`.

Course Miro shows illustrative conversions around 100% → 50% → 25% → 15% → 10%; treat these only as an example funnel, never a benchmark without current project data.

## Modes
- **Manual**: preferred for B2B and early complex sales.
- **Semi-automatic**: product carries user between stages, operator nudges/checks; often suitable for B2C.
- Fully automatic onboarding is a scaling step, not an early default.

## Qualification
Ask only questions that decide avatar fit or materially improve solution/offer. Examples by project may include profession/company type, geo, current workflow, volume, current tool, budget/traffic volume.

## Activation
Deliver a micro-result or let user test the core value quickly. Possible patterns in source: one free unit, short trial, result-before-payment, guided demo/onboarding.

## Payment close
Explain value, why now, why trust the offer, then give a direct payment link/request. Follow up explicitly when payment does not arrive.

## Trials/paywalls
Course recommends short trials and seeing the paywall early enough to measure willingness to pay. Track how many users actually reach paywall, not only registrations.

## First users
Where practical, manually onboard roughly the first 10 paying users / first ~50–100 qualified leads before automating the process.

## Capture
Qualification answers, situation, pain intensity, current solution, urgency, budget/authority when relevant, objection, offer made, CTA, payment/prepayment result.
