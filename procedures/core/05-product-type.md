# Choose validation product type

Choose exactly one:

## manual_service
Deliver the value manually. Best when the output can be produced as a service/consulting/ops workflow.

## simulated_product
Use prototype/demo/fake front/early-access flow to test demand and acquisition before full build. Do not misrepresent unavailable functionality; be clear at the commitment/payment step about what is delivered and when.

## working_product_required
Use when the market expectation or usage loop makes a working product necessary for a meaningful test (common for some apps/SaaS/store contexts).

The objective is earliest credible market feedback, not maximal engineering.
