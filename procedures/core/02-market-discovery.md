# Market discovery

## Course model
A market is useful when there are real repeated transactions. The course calls a favorable early-project environment a **green market**.

## Signals
1. many small players;
2. many transactions / stable or growing demand;
3. easy switching between products.

Additional course red flags: obvious concentration/oligopoly, one player buying most traffic, venture-growth economics that make profit-oriented indie competition hard.

## Procedure
1. Define market narrowly enough to research; list key avatars and exclusions.
2. Find players and current products.
3. Check transaction/demand traces: search demand, reviews, usage, ads, revenue/public company data when available.
4. Check switching cost.
5. Record every signal as evidence, not a score.

## Gate
`pass` when market existence is evidenced and no fatal indie-project blocker is visible. Do not require exact market size if course evidence does not.
