# Product research & marketing contract

## Research scope
Research only what is needed for the contract: avatar, pain, competitive solutions and how those solutions are sold/acquired.

## Minimum source coverage
- competitor site/product usage or demo when possible;
- pricing;
- reviews in target geo/platform;
- video/reviews/community discussion when available;
- traffic/acquisition traces: search ads, social ad libraries, Telegram placements, SEO, marketplace visibility;
- current geo-specific evidence.

## Contract
One geo; one avatar; one situation; one primary pain; detailed solution steps; product type; optional competitive advantage; exclusions; price; one primary benefit; benefit-vs-price logic; observed competitor channels.

## Coherence gate
`avatar → situation → pain → solution → benefit` must describe one causal story. If one link does not follow, return to research instead of writing ads.

## Competitive advantage
Do not invent innovation by default. If direct side-by-side traffic makes a weak MVP hard to choose, seek advantage by deeper competitor/user/trend research. If a similar offer can compete as-is, the course allows launching without novelty.
