# Existing-project audit source trace

The audit workflow is derived from the supplied course transcript and companion
materials, especially the launch troubleshooting, funnel decomposition and lead
onboarding sections.

## Course concepts operationalized

- **One avatar / one pain / one benefit / one GTM**: used by the positioning and
  simplify checks.
- **Wrong channel**: one channel should be selected by demand/audience logic and
  debugged instead of cycling through many channels.
- **Rushing**: traffic/optimization systems should not be judged from a tiny,
  very short run.
- **All for everyone**: broad segmentation lowers funnel quality and consumes
  founder time on non-target leads.
- **“Funnel of fate”**: Product Hunt/friends/random attention do not replace a
  controlled target funnel.
- **Premature evaluation**: conclusions require sufficiently complete and
  reasonably homogeneous funnel data.
- **Decomposition/debugging**: diagnose from exposure through qualified leads and
  payments instead of asking whether the whole product “works”.
- **Early sales flow**: qualification → need discovery → demo/value delivery →
  close/payment ask.
- **Scaling**: a clearer sales funnel, target-lead criteria, payment evidence and
  economics come before aggressive scale.

Exact numeric examples and channel benchmarks from the course remain
`course_heuristic`; current platform facts must be re-verified before execution.
