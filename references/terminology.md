# Terminology

- **Avatar** — one target buyer/user profile or situation-specific segment.
- **Situation** — concrete context in which avatar experiences the problem.
- **Pain** — primary problem worth solving/paying for.
- **Solution** — step-by-step mechanism of the product/service.
- **Benefit** — primary outcome in money/time/risk/other meaningful value.
- **Green market** — course term for indie-friendly environment: many small players, transactions, easy switching.
- **Reference** — live comparable product used as market/product model.
- **Qualified lead** — lead matching the avatar/conditions enough to merit sales/onboarding work.
- **GTM** — selected acquisition route and its launch package.
- **Iteration** — stable test period with hypothesis, plan, actuals, conclusion and one next change.
