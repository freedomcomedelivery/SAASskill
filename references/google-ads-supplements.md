# Google Ads / ASA course supplements

These notes index archived supplementary pages bundled with the course. They are useful context but platform configuration must be verified live before execution.

## Apple Search Ads
Course snapshot recommends testing ASA for mobile apps, one geo per campaign, relevant store-search keywords, negative keywords, and tracking installs/activation/paywall/purchase. The archived page gives an example funnel but explicitly shows that paid trials did not directly convert while organic rank effects contributed purchases. Do not generalize the example economics.

## Google Performance Max / display
Supplement emphasizes having multiple asset variants, audience/search-theme signals for a new product, and checking channel/location/device performance after launch. Current PMax controls and asset requirements must be rechecked.

## Google Ads analytics
Archived guide shows two web paths: Google Tag Manager conversion setup or importing key events from Google Analytics; for apps, Firebase/third-party analytics. The durable rule is: define and test meaningful conversion events before optimizing campaigns. Consent/event UI details are current-platform facts and must be revalidated.

## Keyword research supplement
The archived 2026 article reinforces intent-first keyword selection: distinguish keyword vs actual search query, prefer commercial/transactional intent for direct response, use long-tail specificity when budget is constrained, keep negative-keyword/search-term work ongoing, and evaluate CPC/relevance rather than volume alone.

## CPC factors supplement
Archived article discusses auction competition, targeting/device/time, CTR, ad relevance, landing quality, negatives and ad extensions as CPC/efficiency drivers. Treat detailed UI/auction behavior as vendor-specific and revalidate.
