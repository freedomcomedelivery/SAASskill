# Course heuristics registry

These are **source-method heuristics**, not universal truths. Revalidate when used outside the course context.

- Idea references: aim for 3–5 close earning analogs.
- Attractive check examples: B2C around $10+; B2B around $150+.
- Green market signals: many small players, many transactions, easy switching.
- Early simplification: one avatar, pain, benefit, GTM.
- Search candidate workflow: review ~10–20, launch ~4–5 focused keywords.
- Search demand rough examples: ~3k queries/group B2B; ~15k B2C.
- Early paid budgets in templates often use $5–50/day depending channel.
- Outreach initial base example: >=100 relevant contacts.
- Outreach examples: 40–70% opens, 10–20% replies can be good in some cases.
- First onboarding: roughly first 10 paying users / ~50–100 qualified leads manually when practical.
- Do not conclude from one attempt; up to four iterations is repeatedly recommended.
- Landing teaching proportions: first screen ~5%; benefits/product mechanism ~60%.

Any number here should be emitted as `course_heuristic` unless independently verified for the current market/channel.
