# Audit error taxonomy — source methodology

These are operational checks derived from the course transcript, not universal
startup laws.

## 1. Wrong channel
The course recommends finishing/debugging one acquisition channel rather than
cycling through many. If the product is actively searched, search/software
marketplaces take priority. For a small non-searchable target audience, targeted
outreach is the course's default path.

## 2. Rushing
Do not evaluate a traffic system after a tiny/brief run. Algorithms, channel noise
and lead quality need time/exposure. Exact thresholds are course heuristics and
must not be represented as universal platform rules.

## 3. All for everyone
A launch should be narrowed to one primary avatar, pain, benefit and offer. A broad
product may exist, but the controlled commercial test must still isolate a segment.

## 4. Funnel of fate
Product Hunt, friends, random organic attention or a single uncontrolled launch do
not replace a measurable target funnel. Conclusions should come from the funnel,
not opinions.

## 5. Premature evaluation
Keep the tested population/channel reasonably homogeneous and avoid strong product
conclusions from incomplete/noisy data.

## 6. No decomposition/debugging
Instrument the funnel and find the earliest meaningful break: insufficient
exposure, bad click-through/visit quality, lead conversion, target-lead share,
activation/value delivery, payment close, or economics.

## Sales diagnostics
For early customers, explicitly observe:
`qualification → need discovery → demo/value delivery → close/payment ask`.
The purpose is to understand where target users fall out before automating the
process.
