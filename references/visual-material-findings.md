# Findings recovered from Drive PDFs / visual boards

## Avatar-width Miro
Frames avatar breadth as an economic/channel variable: broad avatars provide scale and many channels but more noise/lower relevance; narrow professional avatars can support higher checks and strong feedback but are harder to source and slower to close.

## Lead onboarding Miro
Explicit sequence: new lead → qualified → activated → paywall → paying. Distinguishes manual vs semi-automatic onboarding, qualification, activation/micro-result, short trials/paywalls, direct payment ask and follow-ups.

## Search monitoring PDF
Adds three search-specific diagnostics beyond the base funnel: actual search terms, share/volume of traffic captured, and ad position/top impression visibility.

## Meta technical Miro
Provides a broad map of pixel/event setup, mobile-first funnel, competitor-ad research, creative construction and campaign diagnostics. It also contains account/proxy/anti-detect workarounds. Those are preserved as source context but explicitly excluded from executable behavior by current-platform/action policies.
