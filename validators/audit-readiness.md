# Validator — existing project commercial readiness

Audit must not collapse into general advice. Every blocking/high finding needs
either source evidence, connected-system evidence, or an explicit user-provided
fact.

Check:
- one primary commercial avatar/pain/benefit for the controlled test;
- current market/reference evidence;
- offer, CTA and visible mechanism/value;
- one justified primary acquisition channel;
- tracked funnel through qualified leads and payments;
- early sales steps for first customers;
- economics / budget constraints;
- sufficient data before channel/product-level conclusions.

Output readiness as a state, not a score:
`not_ready | ready_for_controlled_sales | sales_validated | ready_to_scale`.
